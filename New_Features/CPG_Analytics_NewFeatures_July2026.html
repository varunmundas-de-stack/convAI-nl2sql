What is the CPG Analytics Portal?

The CPG Analytics Portal is an AI-powered sales intelligence platform for CPG (Consumer Packaged Goods) companies. It lets sales managers, ASMs, ZSMs, and admins ask plain-English questions about their secondary and primary sales data — and get instant answers, charts, and insights without writing a single SQL query or opening Excel.

The beta release you are testing today ships six major feature areas. Each section below describes the feature, shows you what to look for, and gives you a short checklist to verify it works as expected.

Natural Language Chat — Ask Any Sales Question

The Chat tab is the core feature of the portal. Type any question about your sales data in plain English. The AI understands what you mean, runs the query against the warehouse, and returns the answer as a table, chart, or narrative — in under 10 seconds for most queries.

Try these sample questions

  • Top 5 brands by net sales last 30 days
  • Which zone had the highest growth in June?
  • Show me SKU-wise secondary sales for Fortune this month
  • Which distributors contributed most to West zone revenue last quarter?
  • Compare primary vs secondary sales for Aashirvaad in the last 60 days
  • Top 3 states by volume last week
  • Which SKUs are declining vs last month?
  • Show retailer-wise sales for last 7 days
💬

Dimension Chips

Quick-insert buttons above the chat bar. Click "By Zone" or "By Brand" to append dimensions to your current question without retyping.

✏️

Edit & Retry

Hover over any message you sent and click the edit icon to modify your question and re-run it — no need to type from scratch.

⏳

Skeleton Loader

A loading placeholder appears while your query is processing, so you always know the system is working.

📋

Large Table Virtualisation

If your query returns 100+ rows, the table scrolls smoothly without freezing the browser — powered by virtual rendering.

AI-Generated Insights

The Insights panel runs eight statistical detectors against your live warehouse data every time it loads. Unlike a static report, these insights reflect what is actually happening in your data right now — no human analyst needed. Each insight includes a severity level, explanation, and suggested action.

🔴 SKU Concentration Risk 🔴 Dormant High-Value Brand 🔴 Distributor Dependency 🔴 Channel Stuffing Alert 🟡 Zone Velocity Divergence 🟡 Emerging SKU Momentum 🟡 Push-Model Signal (Weekday/Weekend) 🟡 Pack Size Mix Shift

What each insight detects

⚠️

SKU Concentration Risk

Fires when your top 3 SKUs account for >25% of total secondary revenue — any supply issue hits you immediately.

📉

Dormant High-Value Brand

A brand that was top-5 last month but has dropped >15% this period. Often masked by other brands growing.

🏢

Distributor Dependency

One distributor handles >25% of a zone's total throughput. A credit block or dispute = immediate zone impact.

📅

End-of-Month Channel Stuffing

Last 3 days of the month consistently drive >30% of monthly secondary sales — a classic EOM push signal.

🗺️

Zone Velocity Divergence

Two zones moving in opposite directions simultaneously — one accelerating while another declines — signals execution gaps.

🚀

Emerging SKU Momentum

A small-base SKU growing >20% — early breakout signal worth pushing distribution ahead of competition.

📆

Push-Model Signal

Weekday sales are 1.5× or more than weekends — field-rep activity is driving orders, not consumer demand.

📦

Pack Size Mix Shift

A specific pack size declining >5% — trading-down signal or competitive pricing pressure, invisible in brand totals.

Test: Insights panel loads with live detections

Must Pass
  1. Click the Insights tab in the left navigation.
  2. Wait 2–3 seconds for the insights to load from the warehouse.
  3. You should see at least 3 insight cards — each with a title, description, and suggested action.
  4. Verify insights reference real data (brand names, zone names, ₹ values) — not placeholder text.
✓ Expected: 3–8 insights visible. Each card shows priority badge (Critical/High/Medium), a description with actual figures, and a suggested action.

Performance & Caching

The portal uses a 3-tier semantic cache to make repeat queries near-instant and reduce AI costs. You won't see this directly, but you'll feel it — common questions return in under 1 second on the second ask.

⚡

Tier 1 — Golden Q&A

Pre-computed answers for the most common questions. Returns in <100ms — zero AI cost.

🔁

Tier 2 — Semantic Cache (Redis)

Semantically similar questions (even worded differently) served from Redis. Returns in <500ms.

🤖

Tier 3 — Live AI Pipeline

Genuinely new questions go through the full DSPy + Cube + Claude pipeline. Returns in 5–10 seconds.

🛡️

Rate Limiting

Each user is limited to a set number of queries per minute to prevent overload. You'll see a friendly message if you hit the limit.

Test: Cache speedup on repeated query

Nice to Have

  2. Ask the exact same question again immediately.
  3. The second response should feel noticeably faster (under 1 second).
✓ Expected: Second identical query returns almost instantly vs 5–8 seconds for the first.
